(S1) I. Khavkine, V. Moretti, "Algebraic QFT in Curved Spacetime and quasifree Hadamard states: an introduction", arXiv:1412.5945v3 [math-ph], 28 septembre 2015 ; publie dans Advances in Algebraic Quantum Field Theory (Springer, 2015), pp. 191-251. Pages lues : 37-40, 45, 48-51, 57-59. Extraits verbatim :

Definition 15 (p. 38) : "With M four dimensional, we say that a (not necessarily quasifree) state omega on A(M) and its two point function omega_2 are Hadamard if omega_2 in D'(M x M) and every point of M admits an open normal neighborhood C where omega_2(x,y) - H_{0+}(x,y) = w(x,y) for some w in C^inf(C x C). (60)"

Remark 18 (4) (p. 39) : "Referring to the literature before the cornerstone results [57, 58] [...], Definition 15 properly refers to locally Hadamard states. This is because there also exists a notion of global Hadamard state (Definition 3.4 in [57]), discussed in [45] in a completely rigorous way for the first time. [...] An important feature of the global Hadamard condition for a quasifree Hadamard state is that it propagates [24, 70]: If it holds in a neighborhood of a Cauchy surface it holds in a neighborhood of any other Cauchy surface. [...] We shall not insist on the distinction between the global and the local Hadamard property because, in [58], it was established that a local Hadamard state on A(M) is also a global one (the converse is automatic). It was done exploiting the microlocal approach"
[24] = Fulling, Sweeny, Wald, CMP 63 (1978) 257-264 ; [26] = Fulling, Narcowich, Wald, Ann. Phys. 136 (1981) 243-272 ; [45] = Kay, Wald, Phys. Rep. 207 (1991) ; [57] = Radzikowski CMP 179 (1996) ; [58] = Radzikowski, Verch CMP 180 (1996) ; [61] = Sahlmann, Verch, Rev. Math. Phys. 13 (2001) 1203-1246 ; [70] = (reference non affichee dans les pages lues).
Egalement (p. 39) : "if omega and omega' are (locally) Hadamard states, then M x M (x,y) -> omega_2(x,y) - omega'_2(x,y) is smooth. This fact is far from obvious, since Definition 15 guarantees only that the difference is smooth when x and y belong to the same sufficiently small neighborhood."

Theorem 9 ("Radzikowski theorem", p. 48-49) : "For a 4-dimensional globally hyperbolic (time oriented) spacetime M and referring to the unital *-algebra of Klein-Gordon quantum field A(M) with m^2, xi in R arbitrarily fixed, let omega be a state on A(M), not necessarily quasifree. (a) The following statements are equivalent, (i) omega is Hadamard in the sense of Def. 15, (ii) the wavefront set of the two-point function omega_2 has the Hadamard form on M or equivalently, it satisfies the microlocal spectrum condition on M: WF(omega_2) = {(x,y,k_x,-k_y) in T*M^2 \ 0 | (x,k_x) ~ (y,k_y), k_x > 0} =: H. (76) [...] (b) If omega' is another Hadamard state on A(M), then omega_2 - omega'_2 in C^inf(M x M, C).
Proof. (a) Suppose that omega satisfies (i), then it is globally Hadamard in the sense of [57] due to Theorem 9.2 in [58]. Theorem 5.1 in [57] implies that (ii) holds. Conversely, if (ii) is valid, Theorem 5.1 in [57] entails that omega is globally and thus locally Hadamard so that (i) holds true. (b) immediately arises from Theorem 4.3 in [58]."
Note 11 : "Results in [57, 58] are stated for xi = 0 in KG operator, however they are generally valid for m^2 replaced by a given smooth function, as specified at the beginning of p. 533 in [57]."

Proposition 16 (p. 50) : "Consider a state omega on A(M), with omega_2 in D'(M x M), where M is a (time oriented) globally hyperbolic spacetime with dimension n >= 2. The following facts hold. (a) If WF(omega_2) has the Hadamard form, then M x -> omega_2(x,f) is smooth for every f in C_0^inf(M). (b) If WF(omega_2|_{O x O}) has the Hadamard form on O, where O is an open neighborhood of a smooth spacelike Cauchy surface Sigma of M, then WF(omega_2) has the Hadamard form on M."
Preuve de (b) (p. 50-51) : omega_2 est une bisolution ; construction de S f = f - P[chi_+ E_- f + chi_- E_+ f] avec une partition de l'unite adaptee a Sigma, identite omega_2 = S^t o omega_2 o S, et theoreme de composition des noyaux (front d'onde) : H'_O o F'_± inclus dans H'_M ; "Such an adapted partition of unity always exists if O is globally hyperbolic in its own right and, if not, since M is globally hyperbolic, any open neighborhood of Sigma will contain a possibly smaller neighborhood of Sigma that is also globally hyperbolic".

Remark 23 (p. 51) : "With an elementary re-adaptation, statement (b) holds true weakening the hypotheses, only requiring that omega_2 in D'(M x M) and that it satisfies KG equation in both arguments up to smooth functions r, l in C^inf(M x M, C) [...]. In this form, it closes a gap present in the proof of the main result of [57], Theorem 5.1 (the fact that 1 implies 3), and proves the statement on p. 548 of [57] immediately after the proof of the mentioned theorem. It should be mentioned that the same gap had been previously explicitly identified and filled in the work of Sahlmann and Verch [61]. These authors merged the partial proof of Radzikowski with the more restrictive result on the 'propagation of Hadamard form' obtained previously in the works [24, 26, 45]"
Note 12 : "The gap is the content of the three lines immediately before the proof (ii) 3 => 2 on p. 547 of [57]: The reasoning presented there cannot exclude elements of the form either (x_1,x_2,0,p_2) or (x_1,x_2,p_1,0) from WF(omega_2) outside N."

(S2) K. Sanders, "Equivalence of the (generalised) Hadamard and microlocal spectrum condition for (generalised) free fields in curved spacetime", arXiv:0903.1021v1 [math-ph], 5 mars 2009 (publie Commun. Math. Phys. 295 (2010) 485-501, reference de memoire non verifiee). Pages lues : 1-11, 16, 18. Extraits :
Definition 2.7 : "A state omega on the Borchers-Uhlmann algebra U_M of the spacetime M is called a generalised Hadamard state iff WF(omega_2) subset Gamma_2."
Proposition 2.8 (preuve) : "The positivity of omega implies conj(omega_2(x_2,x_1)) = omega_2(x_1,x_2)" ; Lemma 2.9 : "For two generalised Hadamard states omega, omega' we have that omega_2 - omega'_2 is smooth iff omega_{2-} - omega'_{2-} is smooth."
Corollary 3.3 : "If a state omega satisfies the Klein-Gordon equation with parameters m, xi on a globally hyperbolic spacetime M and the commutation relations with commutator E_{m,xi}, then it is a generalised Hadamard state if and only if it is a Hadamard state. Proof. The result of Radzikowski implies that a free field state on a globally hyperbolic spacetime which satisfies the commutation relations with commutator E_{m,xi} is a Hadamard state if and only if WF(omega_2) = WF(E_{m,xi}) ∩ Gamma_2."
Proposition 4.1 : "Let omega be a generalised Hadamard state and assume that for n >= 1 we have (x_n,k_n;...;x_1,k_1) in WF(omega_n). Then (x_1,k_1) in V*+ ∪ Z and (x_n,k_n) in V*- ∪ Z." Preuve : "the positivity allows us to perform the GNS-construction".
Lemma 4.6 (n = 0 = Lemma 2.9) : deux etats Hadamard generalises de meme commutateur E ont omega_2 - omega'_2 lisse.
